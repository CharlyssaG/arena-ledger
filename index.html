The Intake

One contestant per line, or paste a spreadsheet (CSV or tab-separated, first row = headers). Anything you leave blank is rolled for you.

    Spreadsheet template

    Fill this in Sheets or Excel, copy the cells, paste them into the box. Only Name is required.

    ColumnWhat it does
    NameRequired.
    Pronounsshe, he or they.
    Traitbrave, cunning, kind, ruthless, paranoid, steady, reckless, showy.
    BackgroundShows up in flavor lines: "a baker's apprentice".
    SectorNumber. Blank = two per sector in order.
    STR SPD SMT STL SOC1 to 10 each. Blank = rolled.
    ItemsStart with gear: knife, bow, medkit, rations, waterskin and more. Comma separated.
    AllianceAny label. Contestants sharing a label start allied (Crew, Pack, Unit).
    Championyes for a past champion (used by the Veterans twist).
    BioNotes, shown in the contestant card.
    ScoreTraining score 1 to 12. Blank = the Overseers score them from their stats.
    SponsorsStarting sponsor money in credits. Blank = based on score and charm (usually 200 to 600).
    PatronName of their Syndicate sponsor. Blank = assigned.

    Prefer typing? Pipes work too: Maren Holloway | she | brave | a baker | str=8 items=bow,knife alliance=Crew
