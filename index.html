The Reaping

One tribute per line, or paste a spreadsheet (CSV or tab-separated, first row = headers). Anything you leave blank is rolled for you.

    Spreadsheet template

    Fill this in Sheets or Excel, copy the cells, paste them into the box. Only Name is required.

    ColumnWhat it does
    NameRequired.
    Pronounsshe, he or they.
    Traitbrave, cunning, kind, ruthless, paranoid, steady, reckless, showy.
    BackgroundShows up in flavor lines: "a baker's apprentice".
    DistrictNumber. Blank = two per district in order.
    STR SPD SMT STL SOC1 to 10 each. Blank = rolled.
    ItemsStart with gear: knife, bow, medkit, rations, waterskin and more. Comma separated.
    AllianceAny label. Tributes sharing a label start allied (Careers, Pack, Crew).
    Victoryes for a past champion (used by the Veterans twist).
    BioNotes, shown in the tribute card.
    ScoreTraining score 1 to 12. Blank = the Gamemakers score them from their stats.
    SponsorsStarting sponsor money in marks. Blank = based on score and charm (usually 200 to 600).
    PatronName of their Capitol sponsor. Blank = assigned.

    Prefer typing? Pipes work too: Maren Holloway | she | brave | a baker | str=8 items=bow,knife alliance=Careers
